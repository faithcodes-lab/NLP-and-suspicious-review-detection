### 4. Reviewer ID & Review Frequency Challenge

**Issue:**
- Reviewer frequency is a key feature in the model  
- Amazon changed the reviewer ID format, making it incompatible with training data  

**Attempts made:**
- Searching for updated IDs: unsuccessful  
- Accessing Amazon data: restricted  
- Matching old and new IDs: not feasible  

**Solution:**
- Used median reviewer frequency as fallback  
- Created a lookup table integrated into backend processing  

**Limitation:**
- Feature accuracy reduced due to lack of real-time reviewer history  

---

### 5. Impact on Model Performance

**Key observation:**
- Removing reviewer frequency significantly reduced model performance  

**Current state:**
- Burstiness feature remains functional and important  
- Reviewer frequency remains the main limitation  

**Conclusion:**
- Limitation will be clearly communicated during presentation
