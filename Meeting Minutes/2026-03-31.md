A key focus was improving model performance through *feature optimisation*.

---

## 2. Feature Ablation and Feature Selection

To optimise feature usage, the team applied *feature ablation*:

- Systematically removed features  
- Evaluated impact on model performance  

Findings:

- The *AI suspicious score negatively impacted performance*  
- It was removed from the final feature set  

Top-performing features identified:

- Verified vs unverified ratio  
- Review frequency  
- Rating extremity  
- Generic word flags  

These four features were selected for model retraining.

---

## 3. Model Training and Performance

- Logistic Regression was used as the *baseline model*  
- Due to class imbalance:
  - Different *class weights* and *thresholds* were tested  

Key observations:

- Balanced weights alone did not resolve performance issues  
- Optimal performance required tuning both:
  - Weights  
  - Thresholds  

Evaluation metrics considered:

- Precision  
- Recall  
- F1 Score  

Result:

- Logistic Regression performed better than other models  
- BERT underperformed, likely due to its focus on text rather than engineered features  

---

## 4. Feature Importance and Insights

Analysis showed:

- TF-IDF features dominated due to their large number  
- Engineered features still contributed meaningfully  

Insights:

- Certain keywords were strongly associated with:
  - Suspicious reviews  
  - Non-suspicious reviews  

- The *“books” category* appeared frequently in suspicious patterns  

---

## 5. Verified vs Unverified Reviews

Findings:

- Verified reviews were mostly non-suspicious (~99%)  
- Unverified reviews showed a higher proportion of suspicious activity  

However:

- Some verified reviews were still flagged as suspicious  
- This suggests:
  - Verification alone is not sufficient to detect spam  

---

## 6. Model Limitations

The team identified key challenges:

- Difficulty detecting suspicious reviews based on *text alone*  
- False positives and false negatives remain  

Supervisor insight:

- Suspicious behaviour may depend on:
  - Reviewer history  
  - External context
