# Summary of Discussion

## 1. Model Performance and Accuracy Issues

The team reported that multiple suspicious review detection models were tested; however, performance remained unsatisfactory.

Key observations:

- Models showed **reasonable accuracy**, but this was misleading.
- The **minority class (suspicious reviews)** performed poorly:
  - Recall and F1-score ranged approximately between **0.16 – 0.25**
- The **majority class (non-suspicious reviews)** had very high performance (≈ 0.98–0.99)

Interpretation:

- The models are biased toward the majority class.
- The models are **not effectively detecting suspicious reviews**, which is the primary objective of the project.

---

## 2. Feature Engineering Concerns

The team identified potential issues with the feature engineering process.

### Initial Approach

- The suspicious review label was partially constructed using:
  - Sentiment-related signals
  - Other derived features

Issue identified:

- Some features used for training were also involved in creating the target label.
- This introduced **data leakage**, meaning:
  - The model was indirectly given information about the target.

Action taken:

- The team removed features that directly contributed to label creation.

---

## 3. Remaining Data Leakage Risk

Despite removing obvious leakage, concerns remain.

Key issue:

- A **sentiment–rating mismatch feature** is still included.

Explanation:

- Sentiment predictions were used as part of feature engineering.
- Even if indirect, this still introduces **sentiment signals into the model**.

Supervisor feedback:

- This may still lead to:
  - Overfitting
  - Artificial performance inflation
- The team should experiment with removing or isolating such features.

---

## 4. Threshold Tuning

The team experimented with **decision threshold tuning**.

Key findings:

- Multiple thresholds tested: **0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.65**
- Best performance observed at **0.65**

Outcome:

- Slight improvement in performance metrics
- However, overall issue with minority class performance remained

---

## 5. Feature Importance Analysis

Feature importance was analysed (e.g., Logistic Regression coefficients).

Observations:

- Some meaningful features:
  - Short review length
  - Low descriptiveness
- However, many features appeared:
  - Noisy
  - Weak in predictive power

Example insight:

- Reviews like *“Great book”* were frequently flagged as suspicious due to low content.

Concern:

- Features may be **too strict or simplistic**, causing misclassification.

---

## 6. Dataset Bias and Class Imbalance

The dataset was identified as highly imbalanced:

- Majority: Non-suspicious reviews  
- Minority: Suspicious reviews  

Impact:

- Model prioritises majority class predictions
- Suspicious reviews are under-detected

This explains:

- High accuracy but low recall/F1 for suspicious class

---

## 7. Review Frequency Feature Discussion

A feature based on **review frequency over time** was introduced.

Purpose:

- Detect abnormal reviewer behaviour patterns

Supervisor concern:

- This feature may introduce **confounding effects**
- If derived improperly, it could:
  - Bias the model
  - Create indirect leakage

Recommendation:

- Carefully validate how the feature is constructed
- Test model performance with and without it

---

## 8. Feature Noise and Word-Level Signals

Analysis showed that many features were:

- Weak signals
- Dominated by frequently occurring words

Example:

- Words like “great” or product-specific terms (e.g., related to books) appeared highly weighted

Supervisor suggestion:

- Investigate actual training data
- Analyse:
  - Why these words are important
  - Whether they are genuine signals or artefacts

---

## 9. Validation Against Literature

The team confirmed that features were derived from research papers.

Supervisor feedback:

- This is a strength of the project
- However:
  - The team should also **experiment independently**
  - Not rely solely on literature-derived features

Recommendation:

- Perform **feature ablation**:
  - Remove features one by one
  - Observe impact on performance

# Key Takeaway

The meeting highlighted that while initial modelling has been completed, **feature engineering and data leakage remain critical challenges**.

The main issue is not overall accuracy, but the model’s inability to effectively detect suspicious reviews due to:

- Class imbalance  
- Weak or noisy features  
- Potential leakage from engineered features  

The next phase should focus on **refining features, improving minority class performance, and integrating modelling with a user-facing application**.
